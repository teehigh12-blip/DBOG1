ABOUT DBOG

People coming together to make a difference.

The Bond Of Greatness Humanitarian Initiative Lagos Nigeria (DBOG) is a group of like-minded individuals who came together with a shared commitment to helping our immediate communities.

Our work is centered on children and youths. We support communities through humanitarian outreach, practical assistance, community activities and initiatives that create room for young people to grow and thrive.
